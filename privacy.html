Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information — and the choices you have.

Last updated: June 23, 2026
Plain-language summary: We only collect the information we need to quote and complete your job, contact you about it, and run this website. We never sell your data. You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains how 24/7 Plumbing & Heating Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information for visitors to this website and our customers in Calgary and surrounding areas. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Who we are

24/7 Plumbing & Heating Ltd., a plumbing and heating contractor operating in Calgary, Alberta. Questions about your privacy can be sent to our Privacy Officer using the details in section 12.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Name, phone, emailTo respond to your enquiry, book service, and send appointment updates
Service addressTo dispatch a technician and provide an accurate quote
Job details & photos you sendTo diagnose the problem and prepare an estimate
Payment informationTo process payment — handled by our payment processor; we never store full card numbers
Warranty & service historyTo honour workmanship warranties and service the equipment we install
Website usage (with consent)Anonymous analytics to understand and improve the site

3. How we use your information

  • Respond to enquiries, provide quotes, and schedule and complete service.
  • Send transactional messages (booking confirmations, technician ETAs, invoices, and warranty records).
  • With your consent, send seasonal maintenance reminders and offers (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • Operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • Meet legal, tax, and insurance obligations.

4. Consent

By submitting a form or booking a service you consent to us using your information for the purposes above. For marketing email we rely on your express consent as required by CASL — and every marketing message includes a working unsubscribe link. You can withdraw consent at any time (this won't affect messages we must send to complete a job you've requested).

5. Cookies & analytics

This site uses a small amount of essential browser storage to remember your cookie choice and run core features. Non-essential analytics cookies load only after you select "Accept" in our cookie banner. You can change your decision at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Our website host and form/email providers (to receive and respond to your messages).
  • Our payment processor (to take payment securely).
  • Scheduling, invoicing, and accounting tools we use to run the business.
  • Authorities or advisors where required by law.

These providers are bound to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, plus any period required for warranty, tax, and legal reasons. Warranty and service records are retained for the life of the applicable warranty. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable physical, technical, and organizational safeguards — including HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls, and limited staff access — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and to notify you and the appropriate authorities if a breach poses a real risk of significant harm.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Withdraw consent or unsubscribe from marketing;
  • Delete information we no longer need to keep.

Contact our Privacy Officer (section 12) and we'll respond within the timeframes required by law. If you're not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Children

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
