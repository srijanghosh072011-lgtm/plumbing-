Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

This policy explains what personal information 24/7 Plumbing & Heating Ltd. (“we”, “us”) collects when you contact us through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It applies only to this website; we comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. What we collect

When you submit our contact or booking form, we collect:

  • Your name (required)
  • Your phone number (required)
  • Your email address (required)
  • The service you need, urgency, and property type (required)
  • Your service address (optional, but needed to dispatch a technician)
  • Any message or details you choose to include

Our server may also log standard request information (IP address, browser type, the page you arrived from) for security and to prevent abuse. We do not use cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

2. Why we collect it

We use this information solely to:

  • Contact you about your inquiry or booking
  • Dispatch a technician to your address
  • Prepare a quote and, if you proceed, invoice you
  • Follow up after the work is done

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not use it for marketing unless you've separately asked to be added to a mailing list.

3. Who can see it

Inside our company, only staff who need it to schedule, perform, or follow up on your service can see it. Outside our company, the only third party that processes your form submission is our form-handling provider, who receives the message and forwards it to our inbox. We pick providers based in Canada or the United States that contractually agree to act only on our instructions.

4. How long we keep it

We keep form submissions and customer records for as long as we have an active business relationship with you, plus the period required by Canada Revenue Agency for tax and warranty records (currently 6 years after your last invoice). After that, we delete or anonymize the records.

5. How we protect it

This site is served over HTTPS. Our hosting provider sits behind a CDN with WAF and DDoS protection. We restrict access to customer records to staff who need them, and we use multi-factor authentication on the accounts that store them.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe a breach has affected your information, please contact us immediately so we can investigate and, where required, report to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what we hold about you and request a copy
  • Correct anything that's wrong or out of date
  • Ask us to delete your records, subject to our legal obligations to keep them
  • Withdraw consent for further contact at any time

To exercise any of these, email us at info@247plumbingcalgary.ca with the subject line “Privacy request”. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
